Lake Toba

The Heart of North Sumatra

Story Lake Toba

Long ago in North Sumatra, there lived a humble farmer who spent his days working in the fields and fishing in the river. One day, he caught a very unusual fish — shimmering with golden scales. To his surprise, the fish spoke and transformed into a beautiful woman. She revealed that she had been cursed, and by marrying her, the spell would be broken.The farmer agreed, and they lived happily together as husband and wife. Before their marriage, however, the woman made him promise never to reveal her secret — that she was once a fish. They later had a son, who grew up strong but mischievous.One afternoon, the boy made a mistake that angered his father. In his frustration, the farmer shouted at him, calling him “the child of a fish.” The moment those words left his mouth, the promise was broken. His wife, devastated by the betrayal, returned to her true form and disappeared into the river.Soon after, the sky darkened, thunder roared, and rain poured endlessly. The valley where they lived was flooded, swallowing villages and fields. The water rose higher and higher until it formed a vast lake. In the middle of this lake stood a large island, which became known as Samosir Island. The lake itself was named Lake Toba, one of the largest and most beautiful lakes in Indonesia.
